Chocolate Banana Shake

Description

This wonderfully crafted shake has been a staple of my household for generations. It's easy, cheap, and leaves you feeling refreshed and happy as a peach.

Ingredients

  • 1 chopped-up frozen banana
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup instant oats

Steps

  1. Add oatmeal and cocoa powder to bottom of blender
  2. Add peanut butter and banana
  3. Add milk
  4. Blend until smooth
  5. Enjoy in a glass of your choosing!
