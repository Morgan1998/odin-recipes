Yummy Yogurt

Description

Yummy yogurt is a dish any one can enjoy. It's refreshing yet subtle flavor makes for the perfect midday snack.

Ingredients

  • Yogurt
  • Cocoa powder
  • Oats
  • Cinnamon
  • Honey

Steps

  1. Add cocoa and oats to yogurt and mix well
  2. Sprinkle on a thin layer of honey
  3. Drizzle on enough honey to best suit your sweetness level liking
